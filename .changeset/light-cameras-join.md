Finish the primitive input wave with `LunaSwitch`, `LunaSlider`, and the `LunaDatePicker` family.

This release adds:
- `LunaSwitch` for binary toggle interactions
- `LunaSlider` for ranged numeric selection
- `LunaDatePicker` with `simple`, `picker`, and `range` modes

It also rounds out the date input story with:
- attached calendar-button positioning
- manual `MM/DD/YYYY` entry for picker and range modes
- picker and range calendar panel flows under one component family
